Executive Summary

  • The week's organizing fact is a removal.
  • Jensen Huang is out at Nvidia — undone not by an earnings miss but by an affair with a woman tied to an accused Chinese spy.
  • The tape answered first: the biggest weekly market-cap decline on record, AMD −8.75%, Dell −7.18%, and nearly $2 billion of retail money buying the dip.

Three threads follow. Terrain: a three-way Trump-Xi-Putin meeting convenes next week in Shenzhen, on Chinese soil, while the Kremlin is down to two attritional plans in Ukraine. Rates: a third straight month of firm underlying inflation has put a December rate increase back on the table — 30-year 5.470%, mortgage 7.27%. Throughput: an FAA groundstop, a 19-hour Microsoft 365 outage, food inflation returning, and a permitting regime that cannot build.

Cluster 1: Geopolitics & Defense

Shenzhen: the venue is the victory

"Trump to Hold Talks With China's Xi and Russia's Putin Next Week", by Marianne LeVine and Vera Bergengruen, puts three principals at one table in Shenzhen — China's tech hub and the corridor Washington has been fencing off through entity listings, a perimeter Treasury endorsed when Scott Bessent backed blacklisting Megaspeed ("Bessent Says Treasury Supported Blacklisting Megaspeed"). Washington arrives as one of three in someone else's capital; Moscow as a pariah restored to co-equal standing without conceding a trench.

"Putin Had Four Plans to Defeat Ukraine. He Only Has Two Left." — battlefield victory and a Trump-brokered deal have both dimmed, leaving Moscow betting on Ukraine's economic collapse and weakening European support. Both survivors require the war unresolved, so a summit supplies the time the battlefield no longer will.

Kyiv's balance sheet is now a domestic U.S. variable. Robbie Gramer and Maya Davis report (EXCLUSIVE) that "Democrats Order Musk to Preserve Records Over Ukraine Starlink Access" — "Senators say SpaceX satellite restrictions jeopardized Kyiv's military operations amid a barrage of Russian strikes" — and "Ukraine Reports More SpaceX Disruptions" backs the complaint.

Calibrated view: 70% probability of no signed Ukraine ceasefire framework emerging from next week's Shenzhen meeting within 90 days — (rationale: both surviving Kremlin plans require the war unresolved, and the report that Moscow's military and Trump-brokered paths have dimmed leaves no constituency at the table for a near-term settlement).

Emma Tucker's 10-Point leads on the same finding under "The Massive Chinese Spy Operation Targeting Western Tech and AI".

"Senior U.S. Soldier Is Arrested in U.K. Over Allegations of Spying for Russia" shows the same collection economy working another customer the same week.

The response aimed at products rather than people: a Senate committee chair summoned Huang and the Commerce secretary ("Senate Committee Chair Requests That Huang, Lutnick Testify"), while Risk Report judges "Huang's Departure Unlikely to Mollify Congress". Congress is treating an intelligence failure as a disclosure failure, the easier hearing to hold.

The curve, not the headline

"Treasury Yields Rise as Inflation Worries Come Back" is half right. The 10-year moved 1.1 basis points to 5.145%, inside noise; the 30-year moved 4.1 to 5.470%, price down 25/32. But the 2-year fell 2.9 to 4.861% and the 3-year fell 3.3 to 4.914%. A front end that rallies while the long end sells off is a bear steepener — term premium, not an inflation forecast. Every maturity from three months to three years fell; the only instrument in that span that rose was the 1-month bill, up 6.2 to 4.012%. Funding, not forecast.

The fundamental case is real. Nick Timiraos and Matt Grossman report in "Fed Forced to Confront Case for Bigger and Faster Rate Rises" that a third straight month of firm underlying inflation reopens the question of how far and how fast; "Broad Price Pressures Firm Up Case for Fed Rate Increase in December" and "Wholesale Inflation Rose Last Month. The Fed's Preferred Gauge Likely Did, Too." supply the prints. The debate is about a hike, with the target range at 3.75%–4.00% (midpoint 3.875%), unchanged week over week and 127 basis points below the 10-year.

Calibrated view: 65% probability the Fed raises the target range by 25 basis points at its December meeting — (rationale: three consecutive months of firm underlying inflation plus rising wholesale prices and broad price pressures, the front end has not priced it — the 2-year fell 2.9bp on Friday — so this is a contrarian call against a term-premium-driven long end, not a market-following one).

Calibrated view: 60% probability the 30-year Treasury yield trades at or above 5.60% before Jan. 31, 2027 — (rationale: the 30-year sits at 5.470% in a clean bear steepener with the 2-year falling, a term-premium repricing rather than a policy signal, and the 30-year mortgage has already moved to 7.27% from 7.20% in a week).

The mortgage channel is where this stops being abstract: 7.27% on a 30-year fixed against a 52-week low of 6.11%, with the policy rate unchanged. Against the 10-year at 5.145%, the 30-year mortgage sits at a 212.5-basis-point spread — a lending channel repricing on duration, not on policy.

翟东升——体系/结构视角

# 翟东升观察：2026年11月14日

本周最该看的不是英伟达，是那条曲线。10年期5.145%，只动1.1个基点；30年期5.470%，上行4.1个基点；2年期反而跌2.9个基点到4.861%。长端抛、短端买，标准的熊陡。市场读成"通胀回来了"，那是误读。底层逻辑是：短端定价政策，长端定价主权信用。政策利率按在3.75%–4.00%不动，30年房贷一周内从7.20%跳到7.27%——212.5个基点的缺口不是货币政策造成的，是期限溢价在向居民部门收税。

这是结构性两难。三个月核心通胀粘性之后，12月加息重新摆上桌；但联邦债务的久期结构决定，每加25个基点，财政部在长端多付的利息远超政策利率本身。不能加，也不能降，于是市场替它加。制度熵增到这一步，利率不再是央行的变量，而是主权资产负债表的残差。

日本一天砸出500亿美元救日元，且有美方罕见的联合行动——这比汇率本身重要。500亿买到一个点位，没买到趋势。美元指数98.34、周涨0.84%，可对中国的10年期利差是352.7个基点。利差在那里，干预就是逆水行舟。所谓联合干预，是核心国替同盟的资产负债表兜底，已是物理天花板，不是政策技巧。

于是深圳那场三方会谈要放回这个框架读。地点在中国的科技走廊，华盛顿是三席之一，不是主人。普京只剩两个都需要战争不结束的方案，所以他要时间；美国要的是把长端的定价压力向外转移。真正的议程不在乌克兰，在结算货币。

金灿荣——美国政治/战略相持视角

宋鸿兵——货币/债务/周期视角

# 宋鸿兵观察：2026年11月14日

本周真正的事件不是英伟达，而是曲线的形状。两年期跌2.9个基点，三十年涨4.1个基点至5.470%——标准熊陡。政策利率中值3.875%，三十年5.470%，中间那160个基点不是通胀预期，是期限溢价：私人资本对"长期承诺"重新开价。前端在赌美联储不敢动，长端在赌别人替它持有三十年。当长端自行定价，央行对远端的主导权就流失了——金融高边疆的第一个缺口，从来不是被攻破的，是自己让渡的。

日本单日投入500亿美元托举日元，罕见地与美国联手，而美元指数仍收98.34、周涨0.84%、连涨四日。500亿美元买到了一个点位，没买到趋势。美元环流的回程是"顺差国积累储备—买入美债—资本回流"，日本是这条回路上最大的阀门；当它必须抛售资产保卫本币，阀门就在关小。干预失效本身就是数据。

从政策利率通向家庭的管道更直白：按揭7.27%，52周低点6.11%，政策利率一动未动，212.5个基点的价差是把久期风险转嫁给住户。德国3.398%、日本2.862%、中国1.618%，英国5.344%反超美国——旧帝国借钱比新帝国贵，这就是三变六反的现场记录。

英伟达史上最大单周市值蒸发，20亿美元散户抄底，美联储同时把对冲基金杠杆与AI估值列为风险。这不是一只股票的问题，而是整个系统的问题：它的股票是抵押品，抵押品重估，回购链条跟着重估，散户是最后一批流动性提供者。茨威格说价值基金里塞满了AI股票——单点故障的教科书定义：人人都以为持有分散的低风险敞口，其实持有的是同一个久期敏感的多头。Anthropic周四以1.33万亿美元挂牌，卖方先讲"少承诺、多交付"，请相信算术：周期的顶部，总是以叙事变成货币的形式出现。

物以稀为贵乃是投资的天道。今天稀缺的不是算力，是安全的长久期资产。
