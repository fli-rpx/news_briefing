Cluster 1 — Geopolitics & Defense

The Strait Is the Position, Not the Prize

Both governments are now striking islands. "U.S., Iran Exchange Strikes on Islands as They Fight for Control of Strait" — Shelby Holliday, Summer Said and Benoit Faucon — describes a fight for control of the Hormuz waterway in which both sides hit islands in recent days. The chokepoint is not blockaded so much as administered by two militaries.

What makes the story strange is the price. "Oil Prices Extend Decline After Trump Signals Restraint on Iran" reports Brent briefly falling below $90 after the president indicated further U.S. strikes did not seem necessary for now — Farhan Rafid's piece, and the most consequential sentence in the paper. "Oil Futures Extend Slide as Trump Indicates Restraint on Iran" adds that futures extended Monday's selloff after the president reiterated that re-escalation is unlikely. Escalation buys a headline; restraint buys an inflation print. At 4.990% on the ten-year with pump prices under $4, cheap crude is doing monetary work the Fed cannot do without a hike.

Calibrated view: 60% probability that U.S. and Iranian forces remain in a sub-blockade exchange — strikes on islands and at sea, but no sustained halt to commercial transits through Hormuz — for the next six weeks — (rationale: crude at $85.36 and Brent briefly under $90 despite active strikes, Adnoc Gas still only evaluating a bypass, and shippers contracting directly with the Houthis for Red Sea passage).

The Second Base and the Bypass That Cannot Arrive in Time

"U.S. Aircraft Evacuated From Second U.K. Base Due to Iran Threat" is an exclusive by Shelby Holliday and Alexander Ward: refueling tankers were moved from RAF Mildenhall in England to Germany and other locations, on Monday. Dispersing tankers is not defensive; it acknowledges that the base sits inside the threat envelope.

"Adnoc Gas Still Evaluating Options to Bypass Strait of Hormuz" — exclusive, Adam Whittaker — reports Adnoc Gas examining ways to reduce reliance on the strait, including an LNG export facility on the country's east coast. That is the right answer to a chokepoint, on a construction calendar measured in years against a risk active this week. The private version is already running: "To Cross the Red Sea, Shippers Email Houthi Rebels. Read Their Inbox." documents shipping companies negotiating passage directly with the militants. When a state's guarantee of safe transit lapses, private parties contract with the armed — the same logic behind "Iconic Saudi Tower Hit as Yemen's Houthis Target Riyadh".

# 宋鸿兵观察：2026年11月10日

今天这份报纸上几乎所有的价格，其实都挂在同一个阀门上。10月CPI环比0.4%，比预期高一个点，比9月的0.6%低——市场读的是前者，不是后者。10年期美债4.990%，离5%只差一个基点，沃勒已经把半个点摆到桌面上。

如果你把美元环流看成一个系统，它的核心问题就在这里：环流的第一段是美联储印钞，最后一段是顺差国用储备买回美债。这条回路上，利率是节流阀，油价是泄压阀。今天泄压阀是开着的——原油85.36美元，布伦特一度跌破90，汽油跌破4美元一加仑——压力却仍然顶在0.4%。这说明通胀不是从能源口进来的，是从回路内部生出来的。而回路的另一端，中国10月顺差破万亿美元。债务人的战争与赤字，由债权人的出口来融资，这是结构，不是政策。只要这个回路不反向，华盛顿就只有一条路：用更高的利率把资本吸回来；而每一次加息，都在替去美元化按快进键。

真正的单点故障不在股市，在信用。Anthropic要按1.3万亿美元估值上市的同一天，软银为AI数据中心的电力设备减记70亿美元、股价自6月腰斩，GE Vernova跌6.37%，CoreWeave的约束是融资而不是需求；CDS成了AI交易里最热的工具。风险正从股权迁移到信用，恰恰发生在10年期逼近5%的时候。1883年胡雪岩败于白银，不败于生丝——控制流动性的人赢。今天，流动性阀门握在别人手里，而最依赖杠杆的那一段资产，正第一个承压。NVDA跌3.20%根本不算定价，治理真空不是三个点能算清的。
